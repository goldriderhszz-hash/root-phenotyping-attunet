# Audit of the withdrawn RhizoVision comparison

The prior workflow made an incorrect polarity assumption. The [RhizoVision Explorer manual](https://www.agron.iastate.edu/shared-labs/files/2025/02/RhizoVisionExplorerManualv2.pdf) and [official source](https://github.com/predictivephenomics/RhizoVisionExplorer/blob/v2.0.2/RhizoVisionExplorer/main.cpp) specify dark roots on a light background: pixels below the threshold are segmented as roots. The source also writes a display-oriented exported mask with **black roots and white background**. Version 2.0.3 changed copyright and license information from 2.0.2 according to the [official changelog](https://github.com/predictivephenomics/RhizoVisionExplorer/blob/main/changelog.txt); the archived 2.0.3 output was checked directly below.

The comparison script instead prepared `255 - grayscale` inputs. All 20 archived validation and test inputs were verified pixel-for-pixel to be exact inversions of the original grayscale images. The four archived batch `metadata.csv` files record `Invert images, false`, `Whole root`, and `Keep largest component, true`. Thus the software received bright roots on a dark background without an additional inversion setting.

The scorer then treated **white** pixels in the exported PNGs as root. With the old inverted inputs, the white pixels resemble roots because they are the complement of the large dark region selected internally. This does not validate the intended root segmentation. On the ten fold-0 outputs at threshold 60, the previously used white-foreground interpretation gives macro Dice 0.47626, while the software's black-foreground interpretation gives 0.01369. The corresponding fold-1 values are 0.48988 and 0.01539. These figures diagnose the old artifacts; they are **not** a corrected comparison.

The prior 0.12219 paired mean Dice difference, its interval, and its *p* value must therefore not be cited as evidence of RootScope superiority. A valid effect size requires new RhizoVision output from the original-polarity grayscale inputs, with thresholds selected on fold 1 and saved black-root masks scored as root foreground. The audit makes no claim about the direction or magnitude of that future comparison.
