# Manuscript figure assets

`figures/` contains the figures used in the associated manuscript and supplementary
information. `FINAL_FIGURE_MANIFEST.json` maps the embedded images to captions and
SHA-256 values. The complete numerical source records are in `analysis/tables/`.

Figure 7/8 and the corresponding supplementary plots are generated by
`analysis/make_descriptor_figures.py`. Figure 9 uses crops of the actual English
interface; `Fig9_provenance.json` records crop coordinates and the median-Dice
study-image selection rule. Retained diagrams without a supplied generating script
are provided as figure assets. The manuscript itself remains unpublished.
